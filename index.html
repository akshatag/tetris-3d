TETRAHEDRIS

Tetris in 3D

Stack the falling pieces to fill a complete 5×5 layer. Full layers clear and everything above collapses down. The game ends when the stack reaches the top.

  • Arrow keys slide the piece across the board.
  • Hold R and press an arrow (or Space) to rotate about the X, Y and Z axes.
  • Shift soft-drops for bonus points, Enter hard-drops.
  • W/S raise and lower the camera, A/D orbit it.
  • X undoes the last landed piece — it costs an escalating point penalty.
